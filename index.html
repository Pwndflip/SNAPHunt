Lecture 3: Static Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)

A Content Delivery Network refers to a geographically distributed group of servers that work together to provide fast delivery of Internet content. A CDN allows for the quick transfer of assets needed for loading Internet content including HTML pages, javascript files, stylesheets, images, and videos.
